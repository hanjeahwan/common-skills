# <title>

## Summary

One paragraph: what this proposal changes and its current state.

## Problem

The concrete problem being solved. State evidence (observed behavior, numbers,
measurements) rather than opinion.

## Scope

What the proposal covers: artifacts, modules, contracts, sequences. Named in a
way a reader can use to decide whether this proposal applies to their change.

## Non-goals

Explicitly out of scope. If nothing is out of scope, write "None." and give the
one-line reason.

## Proposal

The change itself. Enough detail to implement and to review: contracts,
mappings, mechanics, rejected alternatives with reasons.

## Migration

How the change lands: steps, order, intermediate states, link/path moves,
coordination with other work in flight.

## Verification

Checkpoints and evidence that define "done": what to run, what to compare,
what must not regress. Every checkpoint must be traceable to a record.

## Outcome

Only for `implemented` / `rejected` / `superseded` proposals. What happened
afterwards: what was implemented (with decision links), what remains open,
what superseded this proposal. Current truth lives in canonical docs, not here.
